
In-App Purchase Test Cases — Blaze Results
===========================================

Use a sandbox Apple ID (Settings → App Store → Sandbox Account) to test.
No real charge will occur.


──────────────────────────────────────────
PURCHASE 1 — Heat Alerts
Product ID: com.blaze.results.alerts
──────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Launch the app. Tap the bell icon in the top-left corner of the Dashboard.
     Confirm the locked state is shown with an "Unlock" button and the correct price.

  2. Tap "Unlock". In the PaywallView, tap the price button on the Heat Alerts card.
     Authenticate with the sandbox Apple ID when prompted.

  3. Confirm the card shows a green checkmark and reads "Purchased". Dismiss the PaywallView.
     Confirm the Heat Alerts sheet now shows the threshold slider (25–45 °C) and a live zone list.

  4. Move the slider and confirm the zone list updates in real time.

  5. Delete and reinstall the app. Go to Settings → "Restore Purchases".
     Confirm the Heat Alerts sheet opens in the unlocked state after restore.


──────────────────────────────────────────
PURCHASE 2 — Blaze Pro
Product ID: com.blaze.results.pro
──────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Launch the app. Go to Settings (gear icon, top-right of Dashboard).
     Tap the Blaze Pro row and confirm the PaywallView opens with the correct price.

  2. Tap the price button on the Blaze Pro card.
     Authenticate with the sandbox Apple ID when prompted.

  3. Confirm the card shows a green checkmark and reads "Purchased". Dismiss the PaywallView.
     Confirm Settings shows an "Active" badge next to Blaze Pro.

  4. Navigate to the AI Chat tab. Confirm the chat is fully accessible with no upgrade prompt.

  5. Delete and reinstall the app. Go to Settings → "Restore Purchases".
     Confirm the "Active" badge returns and the AI Chat tab remains fully accessible.
